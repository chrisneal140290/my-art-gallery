About Me

The artist behind Motha & Maker

Welcome. I'm Marie — a digital artist based in the UK, creating hand-drawn illustrations one careful line at a time. No AI generators, no shortcuts. Just a stylus, a screen, and a lot of patience.

My work spans food and drink illustration, wildlife and nature, and most recently a series dedicated to the 2026 Winter Olympics — capturing the speed, grace, and raw determination of the athletes competing on the world stage.

Every piece starts with the same thing: a genuine love of drawing. That instinct to look closely at the world and find the visual story worth telling. I hope you find something here that resonates.
